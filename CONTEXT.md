# RageBlock

This context describes the original cartoon brawler being designed in this repo.

## Language

**Cartoon brawler**:
A side-scrolling action game built around exaggerated close-range combat, enemy waves, and readable hit reactions.
_Avoid_: Dad n' Me clone, remake

**Kid with cartoon rage**:
The player fantasy: a kid whose exaggerated anger turns ordinary neighborhood conflicts into slapstick brawler chaos.
_Avoid_: Purple kid, Dad n' Me kid

**RageBlock**:
The working project name for the original cartoon brawler. It names the repo and prototype, while leaving the final shipped title open.
_Avoid_: TerrorThePurple

**Rage eyes**:
The protagonist's heightened visual state: glowing scribble eyes that appear when their anger builds.
_Avoid_: Purple as identity

**Neighborhood weirdos**:
Exaggerated local opponents who make the world feel strange, playful, and hostile without requiring realistic violence.
_Avoid_: Civilians, victims

**Mischievous chaos**:
The intended tone: playful troublemaking with bite, where the action feels rowdy and funny rather than cruel or grim.
_Avoid_: Mean-spirited violence, dark comedy

**Heavy hits**:
The combat feel priority for the first prototype: attacks should sell impact through knockback, hit pause, readable reactions, and restrained screen shake.
_Avoid_: Floaty hits

**2.5D brawler movement**:
Ground-plane movement where characters move left and right across the screen and up and down for depth, while still fighting in a side-view arena.
_Avoid_: Strict lanes

**Rage meter**:
A small combat resource filled by landing hits. In the prototype, a full meter empowers the next heavy attack into a larger launch.
_Avoid_: Purely cosmetic rage

**Block Cleared**:
The prototype completion result shown after the player defeats the sandbox enemy wave.
_Avoid_: Level complete, mission clear

**Schoolyard corner**:
The first prototype arena: a street-corner combat sandbox with sidewalk space, a fence, parked junk, a playground edge, and destructible props.
_Avoid_: Dad n' Me level recreation

**Bully weirdos**:
The first enemy archetype: exaggerated kid opponents with varied silhouettes who shove, taunt, charge, and get launched in funny ways.
_Avoid_: Generic adults, realistic victims

**Simple pressure AI**:
The prototype enemy behavior loop: idle or taunt, approach the player, perform a shove attack, then back off.
_Avoid_: Complex tactics

**Toybox chaos**:
The playful physical mess created by launch reactions, destructible props, bouncing objects, and exaggerated combat feedback.
_Avoid_: Realistic destruction

**Combat Feel Prototype**:
The first implementation milestone for RageBlock, focused on proving movement, attacks, hit feedback, enemy pressure, and sandbox completion.
_Avoid_: Vertical slice, full level

**Programmer art silhouettes**:
Temporary prototype visuals that prioritize readable character shapes, combat spacing, and hit feedback over polished final art.
_Avoid_: Final art pass
