# 06 - Connect Hits, Damage, Rage, And Defeats

**What to build:** Player attacks damage and defeat bully weirdos, enemy attacks damage the player, rage empowers one heavy hit, and defeated enemies count toward clearing the block.

**Blocked by:** 04 - Make Player Attacks Work In Scene; 05 - Add Bully Weirdos With Simple Pressure AI

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Player attacks only damage bully weirdos inside plausible hit range.
- [ ] Light combo hits damage bully weirdos and the final hit knocks them back more strongly.
- [ ] Heavy attacks launch or wall-bounce bully weirdos.
- [ ] Landing hits fills the rage meter.
- [ ] A full rage meter empowers the next heavy attack.
- [ ] The empowered heavy attack launches bully weirdos farther and spends the rage meter.
- [ ] Defeated bully weirdos are removed or clearly marked defeated.
- [ ] The prototype tracks total bully weirdos defeated toward the count of 8.
- [ ] Enemy attacks increase the player's damage taken stats and reduce player health.
