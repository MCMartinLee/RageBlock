# 02 - Build Schoolyard Corner Movement Sandbox

**What to build:** The player spawns in a fixed schoolyard corner arena and can move with 2.5D brawler movement using keyboard controls.

**Blocked by:** 01 - Scaffold Browser Prototype

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The prototype starts in a fixed schoolyard corner arena.
- [ ] The player appears as a programmer art silhouette with a clear side-view brawler read.
- [ ] WASD and arrow keys move the player left, right, up, and down on the ground plane.
- [ ] Movement respects visible arena boundaries.
- [ ] The fixed camera keeps the whole schoolyard corner visible.
- [ ] The controls reserve J or left click for light attack, K or right click for heavy attack, L or Shift for dash, and Space for jump.
